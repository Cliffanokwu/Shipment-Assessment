
This interactive Power BI dashboard is developed using the Dynamic Supply Chain Logistics dataset, which comprises 32,065 hourly telemetry records spanning January 2021 to August 2024. Business intelligence dashboards of this nature have been shown to improve operational decision-making by consolidating multi-source data into a unified visual interface (Few, 2006). The dashboard is designed to support a head of logistics operations requiring daily visibility of fleet risk, cost exposure, and service reliability. Twelve visualizations (including four KPI cards and two slicers) are organized across a single 16:9 light-mode report page, with cross-filtering enabled so that any selection propagates instantly across all charts.
The principal finding is that the operation exhibits a structural delay-risk problem rather than an episodic one. Across all 32,065 shipments, the average delay probability is 70%, three-quarters of shipments are classified as High Risk, and only 23% are delivered within a one-hour deviation window. These ratios remain stable across the four-year observation period, indicating that the root causes are systemic in nature (specifically, route selection, supplier reliability, or operating procedure) rather than attributable to seasonal variation or short-term disruption. Such persistent underperformance is consistent with what Simchi-Levi et al. (2008) describe as structural supply chain vulnerability; wherein operational deficiencies are embedded in planning-stage decisions rather than execution-layer failures.

Business Questions Addressed:

What proportion of shipments are currently in the highest risk category, and how is that trending?

Is the average delay probability moving in the right direction?

Where are the cost outliers: which shipping cost band drives the most spend?

Do weather conditions correlate measurably with shipping costs?

How are drivers performing against fleet behavior targets?

Geographically, where are the high-risk corridors on the route network?
